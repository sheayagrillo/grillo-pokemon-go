# Grillo Pokémon GO app — Quality Check

Run this check BEFORE any content goes live on the app. Sheaya cannot
quality-check everything every day, so the check happens here, before
publish — not after she finds the mistake.

## The check (every item, every update)

1. **Available HERE, TODAY?**
   Is this Pokémon / event / activity actually available in Rockledge, FL
   today — not just "live globally"? If it is only live somewhere else, it
   does not go on the app.

2. **Location — in-person only?**
   Is this event in-person / ticketed in another city (Sendai, Mexico City,
   London, etc.)? If yes, it does not go on the app. (Lesson Nov 7:
   "A Wild Pursuit" Steel/Dragon stops only exist in Sendai + Mexico City.)

3. **Timing — still current?**
   Are the dates and times still in the future? Has the Spotlight Hour,
   event, or raid window already ended? Expired items come OFF the app the
   day they end. Never tell the family to catch something whose big hour
   already passed.

4. **Actionable — can they actually DO it?**
   Read the card as a set of instructions. Can the family do exactly what
   it says, today, in Rockledge? "Catch Teddiursa" is only honest if
   Teddiursa is actually spawning. If it is rare, say it is rare and say
   what to do instead (walk as buddy, save candy, wait for the full moon).
   Never tell them to do something they cannot do.

5. **Source — verified today?**
   Every date, spawn, reward, and window is checked against a live source
   (LeekDuck, Serebii, GO Hub) on the day it is published. No facts from
   memory alone. If a source cannot confirm it, the item does not go up —
   or it goes up marked as unconfirmed.

6. **Beginner-readable?**
   Would someone who does not play every day understand what to do, when,
   and why? Explain the what + why + when in plain words. No jargon
   ("Steel-type PokéStops") without explaining what it means — and if it
   cannot be explained simply, it probably does not belong on the app.

## What happened when this was skipped (Nov 7)

- "A Wild Pursuit" Dragon/Steel cards: in-person only in Sendai + Mexico
  City. The family cannot do them in Rockledge. Removed.
- Teddiursa "Catch Teddiursa": its Spotlight Hour was Thu Nov 5 and
  Halloween ended Nov 5. It is rare now. Card rewritten to say it is rare,
  walk as buddy for candy, evolve on the full moon Tue Nov 24.
- Gastly "Catch Gastly": true, but only at night. Card now says "at night".

## Standing rules (from Sheaya)

- Today-only: only what is live right now shows.
- Rockledge-only: if it is not available here, it is not on the app.
- Beginner-first: explain what each Pokémon is and why it matters;
  evolution chains spelled out; include counters a beginner likely has.
- No "Skip" advice — the family catches them all.
- No ownership language on shared pages.
- Trade Board is shared via GitHub, never saved per-device.
- Vivillon friend codes: none more than 6 months old; living repository.
